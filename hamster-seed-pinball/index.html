怎么玩：顶上的仓鼠抱着种子，左右移动瞄准，投下去。种子撞橡子钉 +1 分，落进底部的仓鼠小窝计分：食盆 20 · 向日葵罐 50 · 正中小木屋 100。一局投完达到本关目标分就算过关，解锁下一关。
关卡与排行榜：共 20 关闯关制 — 5 种盘面轮换（木屑原野 · 瓜子山谷 · 月夜粮仓 · 十字谷仓 · 之字麦田），越往后目标分越高、甲虫越快、钉子越密、风车越多；过一关解锁下一关，通关进度按钱包/游客身份存档。排行榜分三榜：分数榜（单局得分）· 等级榜（仓鼠等级）· 过关榜（打到第几关），一个地址在每个榜只显示最好的一条，每页 10 条可翻页；盘面上旋转的胡萝卜风车针是加长的，扫到的种子会被甩飞，离针尖越近甩得越狠。
惩罚（有奖有罚）：盘面两端是臭臭角（发霉木屑），落进去 -20 分；粮仓上方有一只捣蛋甲虫来回巡逻，种子撞上它会被抢走，本颗作废还 -10 分；落进臭臭角或被抢，连击清零、丰收灯熄灭。真仓鼠狂奔时跑轮甩力最狠，高收益伴随高风险。
升级：每局结算按得分发金瓜子和经验，经验涨仓鼠等级（解锁围巾、墨镜、金铃铛、皇冠等装扮）；金瓜子在游戏左边的小屋里买升级：大颊囊（每局种子 +2/级）· 丰收仓（落格 +5%/级）· 金铃铛（金种子更频繁）· 防虫喷雾（甲虫变慢）。升级和等级都存在本机，关掉重开不丢。
仓鼠特征：盘面正中的大转轮是仓鼠跑轮，里面的小仓鼠会跟着真仓鼠一起跑/睡；两侧粉色的是颊囊口袋，种子掉进去会被仓鼠塞进颊囊再吐出来重投，不消耗；金种子分值 ×2；连续落进同一个格，连击让下一颗 ×1.5；撞满 20 次钉子点亮丰收灯，下一颗 ×2。
真仓鼠联动：转速、跑/睡状态、距离、区块进度都来自 Squeekchain 公开 API 的真仓鼠 Squeektoshi。它跑起来时跑轮飞转、窝心格 100→200；它睡觉时是夜场，种子下落更慢更好瞄，落格 +10%。玩的时候赶上链上新一轮结算，白送一颗金种子。
钱包：右上角可连接 EVM 钱包（仅 Sepolia 测试网）：连接后存档与排行榜绑定钱包地址，并显示你的 SQK 余额、Squeeks 数量、算力和待领 SQK；“链上打卡”向 SQK 合约发一笔 approve(自己, 0)（不转币），对应 Genesis 的 The Tester 条件。不连钱包也能玩，成绩记为游客。
社区小游戏原型 · 数据：api.squeekchain.com · 不下注、分数不兑换任何东西 · 不承诺徽章或白名单。
How to play: The hamster on top holds a seed — move left/right to aim and drop it. Hitting an acorn peg scores +1; landing in the dens at the bottom scores: Food Bowl 20 · Sunflower Jar 50 · Wooden House (center) 100. Reach the stage target by the end of the run to clear the stage and unlock the next.
Stages & leaderboards: A 20-stage campaign — 5 board layouts rotate (Classic Meadow · Seed Valley · Moon Granary · Cross Barn · Zigzag Field); later stages raise the target, speed up the beetle, densify the pegs and add more spinners. Clear a stage to unlock the next; progress is saved per wallet/guest identity. Three local leaderboards: Score (best run) · Level (hamster level) · Stage (furthest stage), one row per address, 10 per page. The long carrot spinner needles fling seeds — the closer to the tip, the harder the fling.
Penalties: The two end bins are Stink Corners (moldy shavings): landing there is -20. A Trouble Beetle patrols above the bins; if it touches your seed, the seed is stolen: void, plus -10. Both reset your combo and the harvest light. When the real hamster sprints, the wheel flings hardest — high reward, high risk.
Upgrades: Every run pays out Golden Seeds and XP from your score. XP levels up your hamster (unlocking a scarf, sunglasses, a golden bell, a crown). Spend Golden Seeds in the Hamster House (left): Big Cheeks (+2 seeds per run / level) · Harvest Barn (+5% bin score / level) · Golden Bell (golden seeds more often) · Bug Spray (slower beetle). Progress is stored on this device.
Hamster features: The big wheel in the middle is a real running wheel — the little hamster inside runs and sleeps with the real one. The pink pockets on the sides are cheek pouches: a seed landing there is stuffed into the cheeks and spat back for a free re-drop. Golden seeds score ×2; landing in the same bin twice in a row makes the next seed ×1.5; 20 peg hits light the harvest light, next seed ×2.
Live link: Wheel speed, run/sleep state, distance and block progress all come from Squeekchain's public API and the real hamster Squeektoshi. When it runs, the wheel spins fast and the house bin pays 100→200; when it sleeps it's night mode — seeds fall slower and bins pay +10%. A new on-chain settlement round while you play grants a bonus golden seed.
Wallet: Connect an EVM wallet (Sepolia testnet only) at the top right: your saves and leaderboard bind to your address, the asset panel (left) shows your SQK balance, Squeeks, power and claimable SQK, and the on-chain check-in sends an approve(self, 0) to the SQK contract (moves no tokens) — the Genesis The Tester condition. Guests can play without a wallet.
Community mini-game prototype · Data: api.squeekchain.com · No betting, scores redeem for nothing, no badge or whitelist is promised.
