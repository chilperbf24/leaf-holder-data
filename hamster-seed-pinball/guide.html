🐹 仓鼠弹珠 · 玩法与详情

HAMSTER PINBALL · GUIDE & DETAILS
怎么玩：顶上的仓鼠抱着种子，左右移动瞄准，投下去。种子撞橡子钉 +1 分，落进底部的仓鼠小窝计分：食盆 20 · 向日葵罐 50 · 正中小木屋 100。一次最多同时投 3 颗，不用等上一颗落袋，连点就能连发。一局投完达到本关目标分就算过关，解锁下一关。
关卡与排行榜：共 50 关闯关制 — 5 种盘面轮换（木屑原野 · 瓜子山谷 · 月夜粮仓 · 十字谷仓 · 之字麦田），越往后目标分越高、甲虫越快、钉子越密、风车越多；过一关解锁下一关，通关进度按钱包/游客身份存档。排行榜分三榜：分数榜（单局得分）· 等级榜（仓鼠等级）· 过关榜（打到第几关），一个地址在每个榜只显示最好的一条，每页 10 条可翻页；盘面上旋转的胡萝卜风车针是加长的，扫到的种子会被甩飞，离针尖越近甩得越狠。
惩罚（有奖有罚）：盘面两端是臭臭角（发霉木屑），落进去 -20 分；粮仓上方有一只捣蛋甲虫来回巡逻，种子撞上它会被抢走，本颗作废还 -10 分；落进臭臭角或被抢，连击清零、丰收灯熄灭。真仓鼠狂奔时跑轮甩力最狠，高收益伴随高风险。
升级：每局结算发金瓜子（首次过关奖励 40 + 关卡 × 3；重复过关不发）和经验（得分每 2 分换 1 点，重复过关照常给），经验涨仓鼠等级（解锁围巾、墨镜、金铃铛、皇冠等装扮）；金瓜子在游戏左边的小屋里买升级：大颊囊（每局种子 +2/级）· 丰收仓（落格 +5%/级）· 金铃铛（金种子更频繁）· 防虫喷雾（甲虫变慢）。升级和等级都存在本机，关掉重开不丢。
仓鼠特征：盘面正中的大转轮是仓鼠跑轮，里面的小仓鼠会跟着真仓鼠一起跑/睡；两侧粉色的是颊囊口袋，种子掉进去会被仓鼠塞进颊囊再吐出来重投，不消耗；金种子分值 ×2；连续落进同一个格，连击让下一颗 ×1.5；撞满 20 次钉子点亮丰收灯，下一颗 ×2。
真仓鼠联动：转速、跑/睡状态、距离、区块进度都来自 Squeekchain 公开 API 的真仓鼠 Squeektoshi。它跑起来时跑轮飞转、窝心格 100→200；它睡觉时是夜场，种子下落更慢更好瞄，落格 +10%。玩的时候赶上链上新一轮结算，白送一颗金种子。
钱包：右上角可连接 EVM 钱包（仅 Sepolia 测试网）：连接后存档与排行榜绑定钱包地址，并显示你的 SQK 余额、Squeeks 数量、算力和待领 SQK；“链上打卡”向 SQK 合约发一笔 approve(自己, 0)（不转币），对应 Genesis 的 The Tester 条件。不连钱包也能玩，成绩记为游客。

💰 经济系统：金瓜子 · 吱吱币 · 体力

金瓜子 🌰（升级货币）：来源 — 每关的首次过关奖励（40 + 关卡 × 3：第 1 关 43、第 50 关 190；重复过关不发）、每日签到（15 起）；去处 — 小屋四系升级、20 🌰 补 3 点体力。新号开局送 50。
吱吱币 （玩游戏挖出来的）：每关的首次过关产出（25 + 关卡 × 2；重复过关不产出），另外仓鼠矿机会持续挖矿产出（见下面「本游戏的挖矿」）；去处一共 6 条 — 升仓鼠矿机（8 级，算力 = 等级 × 40，产币越来越快）· 开补给箱（100 吱吱币，随机出 🌰/体力/铃铛/大奖）· 买幸运铃铛（80，下一局金种子更密）· 换体力（30 → +2）· 换金瓜子（50 → 10）· 买矿工头灯（600，永久装扮）。吱吱币按地址记账，是将来主网奖励按比例分配的依据。
体力 ⚡：上限 10 点，开一局耗 1 点；每 30 分钟自动回 1 点（满格 5 小时）；每日签到 +3，也能用金瓜子或吱吱币补。没体力就开不了新局 — 歇一会儿，或者去签到。
每日签到 📅：连钱包后每天点一次，发一笔链上交易（approve 自己 0，不转币，只花一点 Sepolia 测试 gas），奖励金瓜子 15 起、连续签到每天 +3（封顶 39）再 +3 体力，断签连续天数清零。第一次签到同时满足 Genesis 的 The Tester 徽章条件。

🐹 本游戏的挖矿：仓鼠矿机

游戏里的挖矿靠一台仓鼠矿机（仓鼠小屋里）：算力只来自矿机等级——算力 = 等级 × 40，1 级就有，最高 8 级（算力 320）。产率：每点算力每小时产 0.02 个吱吱币，也就是 1 级约 19 个/天、8 级约 154 个/天。在线离线都攒，但最多攒 24 小时的量，攒满就停，每天回来点「领取」到账、顺便重启产出。升级矿机花吱吱币（150 起，越往后越贵）——这就是主循环：挖币 → 升矿机 → 算力涨 → 挖得更快，产出的币再拿去开箱、换体力、换金瓜子消耗掉，不会只进不出。

🐹 关于 Squeekchain 与这个游戏

Squeekchain 是以太坊上的“仓鼠动力”玩具链：真仓鼠 Squeektoshi 在跑轮 WHEELY9000 上跑出的工作量结算上链，完成一个区块发行 6,900 SQK（总量封顶 21 亿，10% 预挖做协议自有流动性）。本游戏是提交到 Genesis Code 的社区小游戏：把真仓鼠的转速、跑/睡、轮次结算实时做进玩法里。游戏内的金瓜子和吱吱币都是游戏内记账，不是链上代币、不承诺兑换；你积累的吱吱币，就是将来按比例分奖励的账本。

⛏ 挖矿模型：算力怎么算、币怎么分

不是每天固定分，是每出一个块固定发 6,900 SQK。块由仓鼠的真实跑量决定：跑满一个块需要的工作量，链就结算一个块——跑得多出块多、睡着了就慢，所以每天出多少块是浮动的（测试网目前约 290 块/天）。每个块的 6,900 按算力占比分：你分得 = 6,900 × 你的算力 ÷ 全网总算力。算力 = NFT 算力 + SQK 算力：Squeeks 要存进挖矿合约才有算力（放钱包里是 0），等级决定算力上限，两只同级合成可升级（烧掉一只）；再质押 SQK 把算力往上限顶。挖矿要等 9,999 只 Squeeks 全部铸完才正式激活，之前的量不补发。本游戏资产面板里显示的“算力”，读的就是这套链上数值。

How to play: The hamster on top holds a seed — move left/right to aim and drop it — up to 3 seeds in flight at once, no waiting for the last one to land. Hitting an acorn peg scores +1; landing in the dens at the bottom scores: Food Bowl 20 · Sunflower Jar 50 · Wooden House (center) 100. Reach the stage target by the end of the run to clear the stage and unlock the next.
Stages & leaderboards: A 50-stage campaign — 5 board layouts rotate (Classic Meadow · Seed Valley · Moon Granary · Cross Barn · Zigzag Field); later stages raise the target, speed up the beetle, densify the pegs and add more spinners. Three local leaderboards: Score (best run) · Level (hamster level) · Stage (furthest stage), one row per address, 10 per page. The long carrot spinner needles fling seeds — the closer to the tip, the harder the fling.

Both reset your combo and the harvest light. When the real hamster sprints, the wheel flings hardest — high reward, high risk.
Upgrades: Every run pays out Golden Seeds (first-clear reward: 40 + 3 × stage; repeat clears pay none) and XP (1 per 2 points, paid on every run). Spend Golden Seeds in the Hamster House (left): Big Cheeks (+2 seeds per run / level) · Harvest Barn (+5% bin score / level) · Golden Bell (golden seeds more often) · Bug Spray (slower beetle). Progress is stored on this device.
Hamster features: The big wheel in the middle is a real running wheel — the little hamster inside runs and sleeps with the real one. The pink pockets on the sides are cheek pouches: a seed landing there is stuffed into the cheeks and spat back for a free re-drop. Golden seeds score ×2; landing in the same bin twice in a row makes the next seed ×1.5; 20 peg hits light the harvest light, next seed ×2.
Live link: Wheel speed, run/sleep state, distance and block progress all come from Squeekchain's public API and the real hamster Squeektoshi. When it runs, the wheel spins fast and the house bin pays 100→200; when it sleeps it's night mode — seeds fall slower and bins pay +10%. A new on-chain settlement round while you play grants a bonus golden seed.
Wallet: Connect an EVM wallet (Sepolia testnet only) at the top right: your saves and leaderboard bind to your address, the asset panel (left) shows your SQK balance, Squeeks, power and claimable SQK, and the on-chain check-in sends an approve(self, 0) to the SQK contract (moves no tokens) — the Genesis The Tester condition. 💰 Economy: Golden Seeds · Squeak Coins · Energy

Golden Seeds 🌰 (upgrade currency): Earned as a first-clear reward for each stage (40 + 3 × stage: Stage 1 = 43, Stage 50 = 190; repeat clears pay none) and the daily check-in (from 15); spent on the four House upgrades and on energy refills (20 🌰 → +3). New accounts start with 50.
Squeak Coins (what playing mines): Each stage pays a first-clear reward of 25 + 2 × stage; repeat clears pay none — and the Hamster Miner below keeps producing over time. Six ways to spend it — Hamster Miner upgrades (8 levels, Power = level × 40, mines faster and faster) · Supply Chest (100, random 🌰/energy/bell/jackpot) · Lucky Bell (80, denser golden seeds next run) · swap to energy (30 → +2) · swap to Golden Seeds (50 → 10) · Miner Lamp (600, permanent cosmetic). Squeak Coins is kept as a per-address ledger — the basis for proportional mainnet rewards later.
Energy ⚡: 10 max, 1 per run; regenerates +1 every 30 minutes (full in 5 hours); daily check-in grants +3, and you can refill with Golden Seeds or Squeak Coins. No energy, no new run — rest, or check in.
Daily check-in 📅: With a wallet connected, one tap a day sends an on-chain tx (approve self, 0 — moves no tokens, just a little Sepolia test gas). Reward: 15 Golden Seeds +3 per streak day (cap 39) and +3 energy; missing a day resets the streak. Your first check-in also satisfies the Genesis The Tester badge condition.

🐹 Mining in this game: the Hamster Miner

Mining runs on one Hamster Miner in the Hamster House: Power comes only from the miner's level — Power = level × 40, starting at level 1, max level 8 (Power 320). Rate: 0.02 Squeak Coins per Power per hour — about 19/day at level 1, about 154/day at level 8. It accrues online and offline but stores at most 24 hours' worth; once full it pauses, so come back daily to Claim and restart production. Upgrading the miner costs Squeak Coins (from 150, rising per level) — that is the main loop: mine → upgrade the miner → more Power → mine faster, while chests, energy swaps and seed swaps drain coins back out.

🐹 About Squeekchain and this game

Squeekchain is a hamster-powered toychain on Ethereum: the real hamster Squeektoshi runs the WHEELY9000 wheel, his settled work completes blocks that issue 6,900 SQK each (2.1B lifetime cap; 10% premine funds protocol-owned liquidity). This game is a community submission to Genesis Code: it wires the hamster's live RPM, run/sleep state and settlement rounds directly into the gameplay. Golden Seeds and Squeak Coins are in-game ledger points, not on-chain tokens, and nothing here promises redemption; the Squeak Coins you accumulate is the ledger proportional rewards would follow.

⛏ The mining model: how Power works, how SQK is split

It is not a fixed daily amount — every completed block issues a fixed 6,900 SQK. Blocks complete on the hamster's real work: once he has run one block's worth, the chain settles a block — the more he runs, the more blocks; asleep, it slows down. Blocks per day float (testnet is doing roughly 290/day right now). Each block's 6,900 is split pro-rata by Power: your share = 6,900 × your Power ÷ total Power. Power = NFT Power + SQK Power: Squeeks must be deposited into the mining contract to count (idle in a wallet = 0); level sets the power cap, merging two of the same level levels up and burns one; staking SQK pushes power up toward the cap. Mining activates only once all 9,999 Squeeks are minted, with no backpay. The Power shown in this game's asset panel is exactly this on-chain number.
